🛡️ Premium Cars Accident & Passenger Protection

Why are Premium Cars' rental prices higher? The doubled premium rental price helps fund enhanced protection and support. If an accident occurs during an authorised rental, Premium Cars will coordinate the claim and bear eligible costs for repairing the rental vehicle. The company will also assist passengers and handle eligible accident-related medical and emergency expenses covered by the active passenger insurance or the company's written protection plan.

What the company takes responsibility for

  • Eligible repair costs for the rented vehicle after a covered accident.
  • Assistance with passenger injuries, hospital admission, ambulance arrangements and accident-related medical claims.
  • Eligible passenger medical expenses covered by the active insurance policy or written company protection plan.
  • Coordinating with insurers, approved hospitals and repair centres, and guiding the customer through the claim process.

What passengers should do after an accident

  1. Prioritise safety and call emergency services immediately if anyone is injured or in danger.
  2. Contact Premium Cars as soon as it is safe.
  3. Keep medical bills, prescriptions, hospital records and incident documents.
  4. Premium Cars will assist with the insurer and eligible expense process.

Important coverage conditions

  • Passenger medical expenses and vehicle repairs are handled according to the active insurance policy and the written rental protection agreement.
  • Unauthorised drivers, driving under the influence, invalid licences, racing, deliberate damage and unlawful use may be excluded.
  • Any limits, exclusions, deductibles and claim requirements must be disclosed to the customer before booking.
  • Third-party injury or property claims are handled according to applicable law and the relevant insurance policy.

Before launch, confirm motor insurance, passenger/occupant cover, limits, exclusions, deductibles and claim procedures in writing, and have the rental agreement reviewed by a qualified local legal and insurance professional.

Driver & Passenger Safety Policy

Purpose: Protect drivers, passengers, vehicles and the rental business. This is a general website policy template, not a substitute for local law, insurer conditions or legal advice.

Before the trip

  • The driver must hold a valid driving licence for the vehicle class and meet applicable legal and insurance requirements. Only approved drivers may drive.
  • Do not drive if affected by alcohol, recreational drugs, impairing medicines, severe tiredness or any condition that makes driving unsafe. No medical diagnosis needs to be collected by this website.
  • Check tyres, lights, brakes, mirrors, seat belts, fuel/charge level and visible warning indicators before handover. Record existing damage with dated photos and a signed checklist.
  • Confirm the number of passengers does not exceed the vehicle's permitted seating capacity. Everyone must use a seat belt; use suitable child restraints where required.

During the trip

  • Follow traffic rules, speed limits and road signs. Never use a handheld phone while driving.
  • Take rest breaks when tired. Stop somewhere safe if weather, visibility, road conditions or fatigue make continuing unsafe.
  • Do not use the vehicle for racing, towing beyond its approved limit, illegal activity or any unauthorised commercial use.
  • Do not smoke or allow unauthorised drivers to use the vehicle. Follow the written fuel, mileage, route and cleanliness terms agreed at booking.

Accident, breakdown or emergency

  • Prioritise people and move to a safe place if possible. Contact local emergency services when anyone is injured or in immediate danger.
  • Notify the rental owner promptly and follow the insurer's claim/reporting process. Do not admit liability or promise payment on behalf of the insurer.
  • Record the time, location, other party details and relevant photos only when safe and lawful. Do not leave the scene where the law requires you to remain.

Handover and return

  • Both parties should check and record vehicle condition, odometer, fuel/charge level, accessories and return time at pickup and return.
  • Explain any deposit, excess/deductible, late-return, cleaning, damage and cancellation charges clearly in writing before the customer agrees.
  • Collect only information needed for the rental and protect customer identity and contact details. Never store card CVV, UPI PIN or banking passwords.

Owner Tips: Protect Your Business

1. Set a price that covers real costs

Before publishing a daily price, include maintenance, tyres, cleaning, insurance, depreciation, finance/lease costs, taxes, platform/payment fees and a reasonable profit target. Do not copy competitor prices without checking your own costs.

2. Use a minimum-price rule

Planning formula: (variable cost per rental day + allocated fixed cost per booked day + depreciation per day + target profit per day) ÷ (1 − percentage-based fees/taxes). Confirm tax treatment with a qualified local accountant.

3. Separate money correctly

  • Show rental charges, delivery fees, taxes and refundable security deposits separately.
  • Do not count a refundable deposit as income or profit. Return it according to the signed agreement and documented deductions.
  • Keep a cash reserve for repairs, insurance excess/deductibles, downtime and unexpected expenses.

4. Prevent avoidable disputes

  • Use a signed rental agreement, identity/licence verification process, authorised-driver list and clear cancellation/late-return terms.
  • Take time-stamped pickup and return photos, record mileage and fuel, and give the customer a copy of the inspection report.
  • Confirm vehicle availability manually until the website is connected to a secure shared database.

5. Build repeat business

Use genuine vehicle photos, transparent pricing, prompt support and fair loyalty offers. Ask for customer feedback after each completed rental. Never promise a vehicle or a price until availability and terms are confirmed.
